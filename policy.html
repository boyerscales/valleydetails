Size is by the vehicle, not by how dirty it is. A Civic or Camry is a sedan, a RAV4 or Explorer is an SUV, a Silverado, Tahoe or Odyssey is a truck or 3-row. If you book one size and we find another in the driveway, you pay the right size's price, and we tell you before we start.

Two Full Details at the same address: $25 off the second car. Three or more cars: we call you with one price for the day.

When we come to you, we run off your outside spigot and an outdoor outlet, and we take $5 off for it. A two-hour job uses about 30 gallons of water and a couple of kilowatt-hours.

Travel

No travel charge within 10 miles of Elk Grove. Past that it is $1 a mile, only for the miles over ten: $5 at 15 miles, $10 at 20, $15 at 25. We confirm the amount before the visit, never after. Past 25 miles, book anyway and we call you before we commit.

Drop-off is at 9280 Trout Way, Elk Grove, at your booked time.

Booking and the deposit

Booking online takes a $25 deposit on a secure Stripe page. It is not an extra charge: it comes off your total, so a $150 Full Detail means $125 when the car is done.

We take it because a booking holds a time we turn other people away for.

Bookings made by text, Instagram message or phone do not take a deposit.

We text to confirm, the day before, and again an hour before. We do not book same-day; the earliest is tomorrow. Open every day, 8am to 6pm, and 6pm is the last start.

Moving or canceling

Plans change. Here is how it works:

  • Up to 24 hours before your start, moving or canceling is free. Text (916) 753-7822, call (916) 249-0631, or use the link in your confirmation text to pick a new time. If you paid a deposit, it moves to your new time, or we refund it in full if you cancel.
  • Less than 24 hours before, or if we arrive and cannot do the job (no car, no keys, nobody to let us in, no parking spot), the $25 deposit covers the slot we held for you. If you booked without a deposit, we ask for $25 before we take your next booking.
  • If we have to move or cancel on you, your deposit carries over to the new time or comes straight back to you. Your choice.
  • Rain. If the forecast looks bad we text you the day before and move you to the next open slot for free. With a covered garage or carport we come as planned.

On the day

We aim to arrive at your booked time. If traffic or the job before yours runs long, we text you. If we are more than 30 minutes late and you need to cancel, your deposit comes back in full.

You do not have to stay. We text you when we are wrapping up.

Before we start, please take valuables, documents and anything you do not want touched out of the car, and tell us about loose trim, existing damage, warning lights or anything that needs special care. We remove child seats to clean under and around them, but we do not reinstall them.

What the price covers

Normal dirt, dust, pet hair and everyday mess. Pet hair removal is included in the Full Detail and Interior Only.

If a car is a lot worse than normal (matted pet hair, mold, biohazard, heavy trash, sand through the carpet, spills that have set), it takes more time. We tell you before we start, quote the extra on the spot, and you can say no before any work begins.

Stains and odors get our best effort. Some do not come out fully, and we will tell you honestly when that is the case rather than promise it.

We do not offer carpet and seat shampoo, paint correction, ceramic coating, window tint or engine work.

If something is not right

Walk around the car with us when we finish. If we missed a spot, say so and we fix it right then. If you notice something within 24 hours, text us a photo and we come back and redo that area at no charge. We fix work rather than refund it.

We take care with every car. We are not responsible for scratches, wear, loose parts or damage that was there before we started, or for items left in the vehicle. Our responsibility for any claim is limited to what you paid for the service.

Paying

The rest is paid when the car is done and you have looked it over: cash, Zelle, Cash App, Venmo, Apple Pay or card. We never ask for card details by text or over the phone.

Discounts

One discount per booking. The $25 off your second car and the $5 mobile discount are prices, not promotions, so they always apply. A referral link ($20 off your first detail, $20 off your friend's next one), a reply-YES $10 off, or a deal we texted you is one discount, and it is taken off when you pay.

Texts

When you book, we text you to confirm and remind you. If you say yes to deal texts, you can reply STOP at any time.

The fine print

Who we are. Valley Details provides mobile and drop-off vehicle detailing in and around Elk Grove, California. By booking, you agree to the policy above and these terms.

Bookings. An online booking is confirmed when your $25 deposit is paid. A booking by text, message or phone is confirmed when we text you the time. We may move or cancel a booking for weather, access or safety, and if we do, your deposit carries over or is refunded in full.

Prices. Prices are by service and vehicle size as listed on valleydetails.site on the day you book. Extra work for unusually heavy soil is quoted before it starts and only done with your okay. Travel past 10 miles from Elk Grove is $1 per mile and is confirmed before the visit.

Deposit and cancellations. The $25 deposit is applied to your total. It is refunded in full, or moved to a new time, when you cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your start. Inside 24 hours, or if we arrive and cannot perform the service, the deposit is kept to cover the reserved time. Refunds go back to the card you paid with through Stripe.

Your vehicle. Remove valuables and personal items before your appointment and tell us about existing damage, loose parts or special care needs. We are not responsible for pre-existing damage, normal wear, items left in the vehicle, or stains and odors that cannot be fully removed with standard detailing.

Our liability. We take reasonable care with every vehicle. To the extent California law allows, our liability for any claim related to a service is limited to the amount you paid for that service. If something is wrong with our work, tell us within 24 hours and we will come back and fix it.

Texts. By giving us your mobile number you agree to receive appointment texts (confirmations, reminders, schedule changes and replies). Deal texts are only sent if you opt in, and you can reply STOP at any time. Message and data rates may apply. Consent to texts is not a condition of buying anything.
