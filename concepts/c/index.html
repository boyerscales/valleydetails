    Questions

    Do you come to me?

    Yes. Your driveway, apartment lot or work, in Elk Grove and south Sacramento. No travel charge within about 10 miles of Elk Grove. Past 10 miles it is $1 a mile for the miles over ten. You can also drop the car off at 9280 Trout Way in Elk Grove.

    What do you need from me?

    A parking spot, an outside spigot and an outdoor outlet. We bring the equipment, and we take $5 off for using your water and power. Not sure you have them? Say so when you book and we will text you before we drive over to sort it out.

    Express or Full?

    Express is inside and out in about 2 hours. Full is everything in Express plus the deep interior: carpets, mats and cloth seats shampooed with a hot water extractor, and steam on the vents, cupholders, console, seat rails and door pockets. It takes about 3.5 to 4 hours. If there are stains, pick Full. If one will not come out, we tell you before we start.

    When do I pay?

    When the car is done. There is no deposit. You look the car over, then you pay: cash, Zelle, Cash App, Venmo, Apple Pay or card.

    What if it rains, or I need to move it?

    If the forecast looks bad we text you the day before and move you to the next open slot for free. Rescheduling is free. Canceling within 12 hours of your appointment is a $20 fee.

    Is there a deal for two cars?

    Yes. Two cars at the same address: $25 off the second car.
