Questions

Do you come to me?

Yes. Your driveway, apartment lot or work, in Elk Grove and south Sacramento. No travel charge within about 10 miles of Elk Grove. Past 10 miles it is $1 a mile for the miles over ten. You can also drop the car off at 9280 Trout Way, Elk Grove.

What do you need from me?

A parking spot, an outside spigot and an outdoor outlet. We bring the equipment, and we take $5 off for using them. Not sure you have them? Say so when you book and we will text you before we drive over to sort it out.

When do I pay?

When the car is done. There is no deposit. You look the car over, then you pay: cash, Zelle, Cash App, Venmo, Apple Pay or card.

What if it rains, or I need to move it?

If the forecast looks bad we text you the day before and move you to the next open slot for free. Rescheduling is free. Canceling within 12 hours of your appointment is a $20 fee.

Is there a deal for two cars?

Yes. Two cars at the same address: $25 off the second car.
