Common questions

Do you come to me?
Yes. Your driveway, apartment lot or work, in Elk Grove and south Sacramento. There is no travel charge within about miles of Elk Grove. Past that it is a mile for the miles over ten. If you would rather drop the car off, bring it to 9280 Trout Way in Elk Grove.
Express or Full, which one do I need?
Both do the whole car, inside and out. Express takes about 2 hours. Full takes about 3.5 to 4 hours and adds the hot water extractor on carpets, mats and cloth seats, steam in the tight spots and work on set-in stains. If the seats or carpet are stained, book Full.
What do you need from me?
A parking spot, an outside spigot and an outdoor outlet. We bring the equipment, and we take off for using your water and power. Not sure what you have? Say so when you book and we will text you before we drive over to sort it out.
When do I pay?
When the car is done. There is no deposit. You look the car over, then you pay with cash, Zelle, Cash App, Venmo, Apple Pay or card.
What if it rains, or I need to move my appointment?
If the forecast looks bad we text you the day before and move you to the next open slot for free. Rescheduling is free. Canceling within hours of your appointment is a fee.
Is there a deal for two cars?
Yes. Two cars at the same address takes off the second car.
