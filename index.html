FAQ

Common questions

Do you actually come to me?
Yes, that's how most of our jobs run. We come to your house, apartment complex or workplace and do the whole detail right there, with no travel charge within about 10 miles of Elk Grove. If you'd rather drop the car off, bring it to 9280 Trout Way in Elk Grove. Just pick drop-off when you book.
Can I get just the inside, or just the outside?
Yes, it's a real choice on the booking form. Interior only starts at $85 and takes about 90 minutes. Exterior only starts at $75 and takes about an hour. Booked separately the two come to $160, and the full detail is $150 and goes deeper on both, so if you want the whole car, book the full one.
What if my car isn't that dirty?
Then book the Express and keep the difference. It's about an hour, inside and out. Where it isn't enough is ground-in dirt, pet hair, spills, tar or water spots, because those take the deeper work in the full detail. Not sure? Text us a photo and we'll tell you which one your car actually needs.
What if I'm outside Elk Grove?
Inside 10 miles of Elk Grove there's no travel charge. Past ten it's $1 a mile, only for the miles over ten: $5 at 15 miles, $10 at 20, $15 at 25. Past 25 miles, book anyway and we'll call you first. Some days we can make it work and some days we can't, and we'd rather tell you straight than not show.
Do you use my water and power?
Yes, and we take $5 off for it. We run off an outside spigot and an outdoor outlet. A two-hour job uses roughly 30 gallons and a couple of kilowatt-hours, which is under a dollar on your bill. No spigot or outlet, or a tight complex? Say so when you book and we'll sort it out before we drive over.
How long does it take?
About two hours for the full detail on a normal car. If yours needs longer because of matted pet hair or a bad spill, we tell you before we start, not after. You don't have to stand around. We text you when we're wrapping up.
Is there a discount for more than one car?
Yes. Two full details at the same address take $25 off the second car. Three or more, book the day and we'll call you with our best price. Customers also get a link after their detail that gives a friend $20 off, and gives them $20 off their next one.
How do I pay?
When the job is done, not before. Cash, Zelle, Cash App, Venmo, Apple Pay or card. There's no deposit and nothing is charged through this website.
What if it rains, or I need to reschedule?
If the forecast looks bad we text you the day before and move you to the next open slot for free. Covered garage or carport? Then rain doesn't matter and we come as planned. Need to move it yourself? Call or text. No fee.
