# Valley Details: Google Business Profile setup

Everything below is copy-paste ready. Pulled straight from valleydetails.site so the
profile and the site say the same thing (Google cross-checks them).

Go to **business.google.com**, sign in with the Google account that should own the
business long term (not a personal one you might lose), and "Add business."

| Additional categories | Car wash (and "Mobile car wash" if it shows in the dropdown) |
| Do customers visit your location? | **No.** Pick "I deliver goods and services to my customers" |
| Address | Enter 9280 Trout Way, Elk Grove for verification, then **hide it** (service-area business) |
| Phone | (916) 249-0631 |
| Website | https://valleydetails.site |
| Booking / appointment link | https://valleydetails.site/#book |

**Do not put keywords in the name.** "Valley Details Mobile Detailing Elk Grove" looks like a
ranking hack and is the #1 reason new profiles get suspended. Plain "Valley Details" only.

**Why hide the address:** Google only lets you show one if the place is staffed for customer
visits during business hours. A home address shown publicly on a mobile business is a
suspension risk. Drop-offs still work, the booking form already gives people the address.

## 7. Verification (the part only you can do)

For a mobile business Google almost always asks for a **video verification**. Record one
continuous take, no cuts:

1. Show the street sign or something showing you're in Elk Grove
2. Show your detailing gear: pressure washer, vacuum, chemicals, the vehicle you work from
   (Valley Details branding/magnet on it helps a lot)
3. Show proof you run the business: open a job in the dashboard, an invoice, business card,
   or business license/registration

Keep the name and phone identical everywhere (site, Instagram, Yelp) before submitting.
Approval takes anywhere from a few days to a couple of weeks. **Don't edit the name,
address or category while it's pending.** That resets the review.

---

## 8. After it's verified (this is where the bookings come from)

1. **Get the review link.** In the profile, "Ask for reviews" → copy the g.page link.
2. **Paste it into Client Dash → Settings → Google review link.** The dashboard already
   texts customers a review request after a job is marked completed, but *only once that
   link is set*. Without it, no review texts go out. This is the biggest single lever.
3. **Text your past happy customers the link by hand** to get the first 10 reviews in.
   Profiles under ~10 reviews barely show in the map pack.
4. **Photos:** upload 10+ real ones: finished cars, before/afters, you working, the logo
   as the profile photo, a wide cover shot. Add 2–3 new job photos every week.
5. **Posts:** one Google post a week (a recent job photo + "book this week" + link).
   Use the "Offer" post type whenever there's a promo running.
6. **Reply to every review** within a day, mention the service and the city naturally
   ("Glad the SUV interior came out great, thanks for booking us in Elk Grove!").
7. **Q&A:** post and answer your own top questions (Do you come to me? Do you use my
   water? How do I pay?). Answers are on the site FAQ.
8. Add the Google profile link to the website footer and Instagram bio.

## Rules that keep the profile alive

- Never write or pay for reviews, or offer discounts in exchange for reviews
- Never use a PO box or a virtual office as the address
- Only one profile per business
